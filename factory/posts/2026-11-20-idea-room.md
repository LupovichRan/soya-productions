Day 1: Idea Room (alpha).

Bring an idea. For two weeks people show whether they want it with small real acts, not likes. Every suggestion gets an answer from the owner, and the owner gives trust points that make a voice louder.

What works today: share an idea, join in, suggest, vote, see the live numbers. English and Hebrew, on your phone.
What it cannot do yet: prove who people are (there is no sign-in), so the numbers show activity, not proven demand.
