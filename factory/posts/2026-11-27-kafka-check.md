Day 8: Kafka Check.

Paste a Kafka config and see where messages could be lost, repeated or reordered, with a link to the Kafka docs for each point. It runs in your browser and on the command line.

What works today: Checks producer, consumer, broker, topic and Connect settings against 28 rules, each with a link to the Kafka docs; hides values that look like secrets before anything is checked, and sends nothing anywhere.
What it cannot do yet: It reads only the settings you paste and cannot see your code, your processing time or what happens when a broker fails.
