# LinkedIn posts

One file per post, named `YYYY-MM-DD-<app>.md` (the date is the day it goes out). They are made by the Studio from a brief: see `factory/studio/README.md`. The queue is simply the files whose date has come and that have not been published. Every post **starts with the line** `ONE APP A DAY UNTIL SOMETHING WILL CATCH ...` and then:

1. `Day N: <App name> (alpha or parody if it is one).`
2. What it is, in two plain sentences.
3. What works today, and one honest sentence on what it cannot do yet. Never claim demand that was not measured.
4. A link to try it, and a link to its one-pager (road map and the "what would you add?" form).
5. The question from its road map entry, so people have something easy to answer.
6. A closing line: Made by Soycatory, no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Under 1,300 characters so it is not cut off in the feed. No real customer, bank or person names. No numbers that were not measured.
Posting is done through the LinkedIn action in Zapier (a link share). The 10-second intro in `assets/intros/` is uploaded by hand if wanted, because the link share cannot carry a video.
