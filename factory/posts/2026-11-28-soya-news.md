Day 9: Soya News.

Soya the cat explains the world in plain, true words. A slider from 6 to 66 tells each story at your level, from Kitten to Cat owner, in English, Hebrew or French, with a quiz and a question for parents.

What works today: Five true stories a week, each told at five levels from age 6 (Kitten) to 66 (Cat owner); English, Hebrew and French, with a quiz, a word of the week and a question to ask your kid.
What it cannot do yet: New-issue alerts work only where the browser allows background checks (an installed app on Chrome or Edge); elsewhere the news shows when you open the app.
