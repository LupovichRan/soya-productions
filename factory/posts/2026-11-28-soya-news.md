Day 9: Soya News.

A weekly AI newspaper for curious kids. Soya the cat reporter tells each story at the reader's age, from 6 to 26, in English, Hebrew or French, with a word of the week and a quiz.

What works today: Five stories a week, each told at four levels from age 6 to 26; English, Hebrew and French, with a word of the week and a quiz per story.
What it cannot do yet: New-issue alerts work only where the browser allows background checks (an installed app on Chrome or Edge); elsewhere the news shows when you open the app.
